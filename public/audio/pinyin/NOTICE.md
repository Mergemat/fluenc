# Mandarin syllable recordings

Most recordings come from the
[AllSet Learning Chinese Pronunciation Wiki pinyin chart](https://resources.allsetlearning.com/chinese/pronunciation/Pinyin_chart),

[CC BY-NC-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/3.0/).
The processed files in this folder are adaptations and keep that license. You
may share and change them with attribution, for non-commercial use only, under
the same license. The repository's GPL license does not cover them.

The `nü3` and `lü4` fallbacks come from
[`davinfifield/mp3-chinese-pinyin-sound`](https://github.com/davinfifield/mp3-chinese-pinyin-sound),
which dedicates its recordings to the public domain under The Unlicense
(see `UNLICENSE.txt`).

All recordings are processed to remove inconsistent leading/trailing silence,
add consistent playback padding, filter sub-55 Hz rumble, and normalize loudness.
