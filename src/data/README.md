# Curriculum sources

- `character-keywords.reviewed.json`: one selected pronunciation and English mnemonic keyword for every curriculum character. Each choice records an exact CC-CEDICT reading and sense, plus an editorial note. These are AI-assisted editorial choices, checked against the dictionary; they are not a human linguist certification or a reproduction of Mandarin Blueprint's course.
- `dictionary.source.json`: filtered CC-CEDICT snapshot covering course characters, words, and cited phrase components and cross-references. Published by [MDBG / CC-CEDICT](https://www.mdbg.net/chinese/dictionary?page=cedict), downloaded from the URL recorded in the file. The upstream publication timestamp and complete-download SHA-256 are preserved. Dictionary readings and senses are retained verbatim.
- `word-meanings.reviewed.json`: an explicit decision for every entry in the frequency list. Accepted entries select a teaching meaning and pronunciation with exact dictionary evidence, or ordered dictionary-backed components for a transparent phrase. Excluded entries record why they are unsuitable or unsupported. These are AI-assisted editorial reviews, not a human linguist certification. A character's mnemonic keyword does not determine a word's meaning.
- `reviewed-compositions.json`: reviewed mnemonic component choices; see [decomposition audit](../../docs/research/character-decompositions.md).
- `sentences.authored.json`: sentences written for the course. They keep their hand-checked pinyin and meaning.
- `sentences.generated.json`: generated by `bun run data:sentences` from the authored sentences and [Tatoeba](https://tatoeba.org) Mandarin–English pairs. A Tatoeba sentence is kept when it has 4–16 hanzi, only simplified curriculum characters, at least three curriculum words, and at most one stretch of hanzi no curriculum word covers. Each one is anchored to its least frequent word; up to three per anchor are kept, preferring ones near nine hanzi with common support words. Pinyin comes from pinyin-pro in context, using a word's reviewed reading where they agree. Translations are Tatoeba's, not edited. A Tatoeba sentence's `source` is its sentence number.
- `curriculum.generated.json` and `hanzi-index.generated.json`: generated by `bun run data:sync`. The frequency CSV supplies spelling and order only. Neither meanings nor pronunciations come from its gloss columns or dictionary order. Generation fails on missing or duplicate reviews, absent source evidence, mismatched pronunciation, or invalid phrase composition. Word meanings are explicit fields, never comma- or semicolon-truncated definitions.

For a new entry, add its source evidence to the pinned snapshot and author its review decision before running `bun run data:sync`. Select a clear teaching sense for the intended use and preserve the reading's other dictionary senses. For a transparent phrase, cite its ordered components instead of inventing a whole-word dictionary entry. The narrow `reduplication-neutral` rule is limited to a repeated monosyllabic verb; see the [CC-CEDICT pronunciation specification](https://cc-cedict.org/wiki/syntax_v2). Source membership validates evidence, not the quality of an English paraphrase. Ambiguous choices still require editorial investigation.
