# Curriculum sources

- `character-keywords.reviewed.json`: one selected pronunciation and English mnemonic keyword for every curriculum character. Each choice records an exact CC-CEDICT reading and sense, plus an editorial note. These are AI-assisted editorial choices, checked against the dictionary; they are not a human linguist certification.
- `dictionary.source.json`: filtered CC-CEDICT snapshot covering course characters, words, and cited phrase components and cross-references. Published by [MDBG / CC-CEDICT](https://www.mdbg.net/chinese/dictionary?page=cedict), downloaded from the URL recorded in the file. The upstream publication timestamp and complete-download SHA-256 are preserved. Dictionary readings and senses are retained verbatim. Entries listed under `supplement` come from the later download recorded there; the rest keep the original snapshot.
- `words.supplement.json`: words the course teaches beyond the frequency list, such as 四 and 汉语, which it lacks. They follow the list in order, ranked and numbered from 1001 by position, so the file is append-only.
- `word-meanings.reviewed.json`: an explicit decision for every entry in the frequency list. Accepted entries select a teaching meaning and pronunciation with exact dictionary evidence, or ordered dictionary-backed components for a transparent phrase. Excluded entries record why they are unsuitable or unsupported. These are AI-assisted editorial reviews, not a human linguist certification. A character's mnemonic keyword does not determine a word's meaning.
- `secondary-meanings.reviewed.json`: optional compact "also" cues shared by character, word and review cards. Each cue selects an exact dictionary sense for a spelling and pronunciation; it is shown only while that evidence is present on the item. Absence means no compact alternate has been selected, not that the entry has no other senses. Author a natural cue with any necessary usage or domain restriction, then run `bun test src/lib/meaning.test.ts` to check its evidence and identity. Dictionary order and stripped parenthetical labels never select a cue. Library details retain full sense restrictions, omit standalone dictionary metadata, and deduplicate equivalent entries. These are AI-assisted editorial choices based on the pinned snapshot.
- `reviewed-compositions.json`: reviewed mnemonic component choices. Each records its sources and confidence.
- `component-notes.authored.json`: optional learner guidance for assembling unusual glyph shapes. Each note records the exact ordered components it explains; generation rejects a note when those parts change. Whole-shape lessons have no assembly notes. Source jargon, review confidence, and replacement history stay in `reviewed-compositions.json` and never select learner copy.
- `sentences.authored.json`: sentences written for the course. They keep their hand-checked pinyin and meaning.
- `sentences.reviewed.json`: explicitly selected [Tatoeba](https://tatoeba.org) Mandarin–English examples. Each entry pins its original Chinese, reviewed pinyin and English, source sentence number, and inclusion reason. Review covers naturalness, standalone usefulness, contextual pronunciation and translation fidelity. Rewritten Chinese belongs in the authored file under a new authored ID. These are AI-assisted editorial reviews, not a native-speaker certification.
- `sentences.generated.json`: materialized locally by `bun run data:sentences` from the authored and reviewed files. Generation preserves their pinyin and English, checks source identities and curriculum coverage, and rejects exact duplicates or unresolved pronoun/quantity templates. An intentional teaching contrast needs an explicit, reasoned `allowSimilarTo` reference. Imported examples retain 4–16 hanzi, at least three curriculum words, and at most one uncovered stretch. Each sentence is anchored to its least frequent word. No new sentences are downloaded or selected by length, dictionary order, or automatic pinyin guesses; adding imported content requires review first. A Tatoeba sentence's `source` is its sentence number.
- `curriculum.generated.json` and `hanzi-index.generated.json`: generated by `bun run data:sync`. The frequency CSV and the supplementary list supply spelling and order only. Neither meanings nor pronunciations come from its gloss columns or dictionary order. Generation fails on missing or duplicate reviews, absent source evidence, mismatched pronunciation, or invalid phrase composition. Word meanings are explicit fields, never comma- or semicolon-truncated definitions.

For a new entry, add its source evidence to the pinned snapshot and author its review decision before running `bun run data:sync`. Select a clear teaching sense for the intended use and preserve the reading's other dictionary senses. For a transparent phrase, cite its ordered components instead of inventing a whole-word dictionary entry. The narrow `reduplication-neutral` rule is limited to a repeated monosyllabic verb; see the [CC-CEDICT pronunciation specification](https://cc-cedict.org/wiki/syntax_v2). Source membership validates evidence, not the quality of an English paraphrase. Ambiguous choices still require editorial investigation.
