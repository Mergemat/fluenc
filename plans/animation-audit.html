How to read a curve, in 5 seconds

All three dots take the same time. The only difference is when they move. UI should jump right away and then settle.

ease-in starts slow, feels laggy. Avoid in UI.
ease-out (yours) starts fast, feels instant. Right for things appearing.
linear robotic. Only for timers and progress bars.

Reduce Motion removes too much

worth doingevery card, for those users

If someone turns on Reduce Motion on their phone, the app stops every animation, including soft fades that don't move anything. So the answer snaps in like a glitch.

Reduce Motion means "don't move things around", not "no fades". Keep fades and color changes; drop slides, flips, throws and scales.

Turn on slow motion to see the difference. The same fix covers the words popover, the write dialog, the pinyin reveal and the lesson dim.
