# Fluenc learning context

Fluenc presents Hanzi Movie Method learning as separate, self-paced activities. Review, new lessons, and comprehensible reading never force the learner into one timed session.

## Learning activities

**Lesson**:
One small Build unit that introduces the next dependency-ready character or word. A learner opens and completes lessons independently, one at a time.
_Avoid_: Session stage, new-card queue, 30-minute block

**Review**:
Fast retrieval practice for material the learner has already understood. Due pronunciation associations, characters, words, and sentences share one independent Review destination.
_Avoid_: Learning, test, lesson stage

**Build**:
Learning new material in dependency order: pronunciation associations, character components, a character and its common meaning, then words made from known characters.
_Avoid_: Timed stage, bulk study session

**Get**:
An independent reading activity with messages made almost entirely from material the learner already knows. Sentences that use the newest word come first. An understood sentence is shadowed before it enters Review for spoken retrieval.
_Avoid_: Session reward, leftover time, locked content

**Shadowing**:
Hearing a sentence and saying it aloud right after, about five times. Required at least three times before a sentence joins Review. Never graded.
_Avoid_: Pronunciation test, listening quiz

**Focus**:
Words and sentences the learner wants taught next, usually from a class they take alongside Fluenc. Lessons teach focus first, starting with the characters a focus word still needs; focus sentences come first in Get. A focus entry with a course spelling is that course item; otherwise it becomes the learner's own entry, as long as every character is in the course. Added with `bun run focus`.
_Avoid_: Custom deck, import, override

**Listen**:
An independent, hands-free activity that plays sentences the learner has already shadowed, each followed by a pause to say it back. Meant for the gaps in a day.
_Avoid_: Podcast, review mode

## Memory system

**Pronunciation Code**:
The learner's permanent mapping from a pinyin initial to an actor, a final to a familiar set, and a tone to a room or area.
_Avoid_: Codebook deck, prerequisite test

**Hanzi Movie**:
One imagined scene in which the pronunciation actor uses the character's component props in the tone room of the final set to express one common meaning.
_Avoid_: Written story, mnemonic notes

**Prop**:
A permanent concrete image assigned to a recurring character component.
_Avoid_: Radical definition, one-off object

**Living Link**:
An optional short association used only when a word made from known characters does not stick through meaning and logic alone.
_Avoid_: Required story, word definition

**Introduced material**:
Material the learner has understood during Build and can now review. Introduction does not mean long-term mastery.
_Avoid_: Mastered, completed
